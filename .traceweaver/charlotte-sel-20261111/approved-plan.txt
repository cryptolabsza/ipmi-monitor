Confirmed collector defects
1. Wrong log selected: RedfishClient.get_sel_entries tests "SEL" against the whole URI. /Managers/Self/LogServices/AuditLog and EventLog therefore match through "Self". The first nonempty unrelated log can be returned instead of the hardware SEL.
2. Pagination ignored: the deployed reader returns the first Members list without following Members@odata.nextLink. Later hardware records can be missed despite the collector running normally.
3. Redfish completeness is not native completeness: several BMCs expose stale/filtered/windowed Redfish SEL views. Correcting service selection and pagination alone cannot prove capture of all native records.
4. Collection health is distinct from reachability: the connectivity checker updates is_reachable and last_check without refreshing cached power status/event counts. Overall /health is not a per-host SEL success proof.
5. Native health transport is fixed to cipher17, which fails on CCC90 even though cipher3 native commands work.
6. Collector exceptions include command-line password arguments. Captured diagnostic evidence was redacted; production logging requires correction and managed credential exposure handling.

Dependencies and risks
Use existing Python, ipmitool and application dependencies; no new service/provider key is expected. Full native backfill costs more controller/network time than one Redfish page, so bound concurrency/timeouts and verify scheduler progress. Historical backfill must not generate an alert flood. Native clears cannot restore data onto the BMC, hence dual verified archives and fresh identity/CAS guards are prerequisites. Clock corrections change future event interpretation and must preserve original historical timestamps. CCC94 may require site intervention; no controller reset, chassis power cycle, firmware or workload intervention is covered by this plan.
